Welcome to ReadEZ

ReadEZ screens for visual stress (pattern-related visual discomfort) and finds the colour that helps a patient most: the colour of a ReadEZ overlay or reading guide, of ReadEZ tinted lenses, or of a computer screen.

The aim of the screening is:

  • to identify those individuals who are likely to benefit from the use of colour, and
  • to determine the optimum colour for ReadEZ overlays, ReadEZ lenses and computer screens.

The tests

  1. Baseline symptoms without colour
  2. Baseline rate of reading without colour (the Wilkins Rate of Reading test)
  3. Determination of the preferred colour: automatic or manual, for overlays, lenses or a computer screen
  4. Rate of reading with the preferred colour
  5. Symptoms with the preferred colour
