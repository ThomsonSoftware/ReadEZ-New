Running a test

Select a patient, then choose a test on the Tests page:

TestWhat it does
BaselineSymptoms and rate of reading on a white background.
OverlaysThe colour for an overlay or reading guide: two passages side by side, each surrounded by an overlay colour.
LensesThe colour for tinted lenses: the whole screen coloured, one colour after the other, in a darkened room.
ComputerThe colour for a computer screen: as Lenses, with the room lit.

Choose the colour test method (automatic or manual) and whether rate of reading and symptoms with the colour follow straight on. Tests can be switched off in Settings > Admin.
