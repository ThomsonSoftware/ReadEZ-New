Colour calibration

ReadEZ may be used to determine the optimum colour for ReadEZ overlays / reading guides, ReadEZ tinted lenses and computer screens. In each case it is very important that the colours displayed on the screen match the overlays and lenses. Displays reproduce colour differently, so check the colours on the screen the patient uses before testing (with two screens, on the patient's screen).

The Colours page shows the three sets – overlays, light lens tints and dark lens tints, each with 12 colours (A – L) and grey – with a tick and date for each colour checked on this screen.

Checking a colour

  1. Click Check colours (or a single colour).
  2. Hold the overlay on white paper (or the lens) next to the colour shown on the patient's screen.
  3. If they match, confirm. If not, choose the closest of the colours offered; a few choices find the match.

The top bar shows how many colours have been checked; the Tests page warns if some have not.
