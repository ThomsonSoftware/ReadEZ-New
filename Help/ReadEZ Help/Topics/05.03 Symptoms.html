Symptoms

The patient looks at the passage of text (on white for the baseline, on the chosen colour afterwards). Text, font, size, line spacing and number of rows can be changed beside the questions; choose settings that bring out any visual stress symptoms.

Having allowed adequate time for symptoms to develop, ask the four questions and grade each from 0 (none) to 5 (severe):

  1. Do the words appear to move, wobble or flicker?
  2. Do the words look too close together?
  3. Does the page look too bright or dazzling?
  4. Does it hurt your eyes to look at the page?

Keys: 0 – 5 grade the highlighted question, Up/Down move between questions, Enter goes on. Other symptoms can be typed in the notes.
