Determination of the preferred colour

Automatic

ReadEZ compares the colours in pairs and the patient says which is more comfortable. Three interleaved staircases (starting from colours A, E and H) converge on the preferred colour in about 33 presentations; the leading colours are then compared with each other and the winner with grey. For lenses and computer the light and dark tints are also compared.

  • Overlays: two passages side by side. The patient says left or right (keys ←/→).
  • Lenses / Computer: the whole screen shows colour 1, then colour 2 (presentation time in Settings, adjustable during the test), then white. The patient says first or second (←/→); R repeats the pair.

The colour wheel shows how often each colour was chosen: a single long spoke means a clear preference; many short spokes mean the patient was inconsistent.

Manual

Choose the colours yourself from the colour wheels (outer ring: light tints, inner ring: dark tints) and select the preferred one. Useful for a quick assessment or when the patient cannot complete the automatic test. A bespoke colour can also be chosen.
