Patients

Search by name, reference or date of birth, and filter by organisation (school, practice …). Select a patient to see their details and tests.

  • New patient: title, names, date of birth, reference, contact details and organisation.
  • Edit / Delete: deleting a patient also deletes their tests.
  • Double-click a test to open it on the Results screen; Edit > Move the test to another patient corrects a test saved for the wrong patient.
  • Edit > Organisations and Edit > Screeners keep the lists used on the patient and test screens.
