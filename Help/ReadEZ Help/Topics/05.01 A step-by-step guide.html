A step-by-step guide

  1. Baseline: symptoms without colour, then rate of reading without colour.
  2. Colour test for overlays, lenses or computer: find the preferred colour.
  3. Rate of reading with the preferred colour.
  4. Symptoms with the preferred colour.

The Results page then compares the colour test with the baseline. An increase in reading speed of 5% or more, fewer errors and fewer symptoms suggest the colour helps. If grey was preferred to the chosen colour, colour may be of little benefit.

The program is a screening tool. Results should be interpreted together with the patient's history and an eye examination.
